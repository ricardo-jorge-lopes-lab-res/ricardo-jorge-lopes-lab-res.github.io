A productive week collecting sound samples from passive audio loggers, for our ongoing project on soundscapes and on the evaluation of birdsong for the long term monitoring of avian abundance in Cabo Verde. Many thanks for the help of the NGO [Project Biodiversity](https://https://www.projectbiodiversity.org), especially Albert, Maldini, Dorivaldo and Bruno.
