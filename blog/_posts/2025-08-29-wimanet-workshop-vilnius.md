It was a joy to attend the WIMANET Workshop and witness how networking can provide so many opportunities for advancing our knowledge in Haemosporidians. Also glad to co-organize with Luz García-Longoria (University of Extremadura, Badajoz, Spain) the WG1 (Genomics) session, where we discussed past and future training opportunities, the impact of short term missions and developments on genomics of haemosporidians.
