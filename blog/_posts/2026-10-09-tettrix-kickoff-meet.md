[TETTRIX](https://tettrix.eu), the new Horizon Europe project coordinated by [CETAF](https://cetaf.org), officially started on 1 September 2026. Bringing together 17 partners, including the [University of Lisbon](https://www.ulisboa.pt/en) and two affiliated entities from 10 European countries, the four-year project will strengthen Europe’s taxonomic capacity and help build a more integrated, skilled and policy-ready taxonomic system.

The Kick-off Meeting was held at Brussels, 7-9 October 2026 at the Royal Belgian Institute of Natural Sciences (RBINS), marking the beginning of the project’s collaborative work. The University of Lisbon will be responsible for the Work package focused on Ethical, social & economic aspects and I will lead the ambitious task of developing a European Certification System for Taxonomy Training, under the Work Package on advancing Taxonomic Capacity.
