My name is Francisco Catarino, and I am a BIODIV PhD student at the University of Lisbon. I otained a BSc in Biology and a MSc in Biodiversity, Genetics and Conservation from the University of Porto.

My PhD project aims to improve the study of Artificial Selection in Domesticated Birds. I address the underrepresentation of domesticated birds in Natural History collections by developing and implementing an optimized Extended Specimen framework specifically designed for domesticated avian breeds, bridging natural history collections science and domestication biology.
