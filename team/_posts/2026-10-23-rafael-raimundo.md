Rafael is a MSc student in Conservation Biology at the University of Lisbon, jointly supervised with [Vitor Sousa](https://www.ce3c.pt/pt/investigacao/grupos-de-investigacao/perspectivas-evolutivas-em-um-mundo-em-transformacao/evolutionary-genomics-and-bioinformatics/vitor-sousa).

His research focuses on the Demographic History of the Azores Bullfinch, using genomics to estimate historic effective population sizes.
